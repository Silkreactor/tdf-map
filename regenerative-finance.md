## Useful checks

- Recover actual funding and proposal status. — Existing finance / proposal owners. Evidence: Receipts, restrictions, duties and decisions, with private records retained privately.
- Establish applicant and signing authority. — Relevant entity / decision owner. Evidence: The eligible authorised recipient for one chosen commitment.
- Recover biochar’s current project evidence. — Current project / land owner. Evidence: Actual proposal, equipment status, operating capacity and cost boundaries.
- Review a dated call only against a real project. — Existing funding owner / capable delivery team. Evidence: Current official notice and a reasoned fit or no-fit decision.
- Screen the FarmTree offer against a practical land question. — Matthijs / willing land practitioner. Evidence: Input/method/terms fit; any introduction remains a separate next action.
- Choose and document a maintainable ecological result. — Real practitioner / reviewer. Evidence: Useful task, baseline, permitted evidence and continuing maintainer.
- Keep instruments and earned income distinct. — Finance owner / prospective operator. Evidence: A decision record separating awards, loans, future services and actual revenue.
