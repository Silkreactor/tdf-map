# AI and tech revenue — v4.1

11 October 2026. Public review snapshot; connected format, not a new factual audit.

What could someone use or pay for, and what would it take to deliver reliably?

Reliable coworking and a useful small result can start with existing resources. Future compute, support and learning offers need user evidence, an operator and workable terms.

## Connected diagram

- Reliable coworking → **Improve an actual work experience** → First useful result (proposed)
- First useful result → **Experience can inform a gathering** → November experiment (proposed)
- Future compute or hosting → **Capacity needs accountable operation** → Operator and agreement (dependency)
- Operator and agreement → **Obligations shape viability** → Demand, income and costs (dependency)
- Demand, income and costs → **Test actual use and costs** → One bounded pilot (proposed)
